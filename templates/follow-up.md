# 跟进邮件（首封后 3–5 个工作日无回复）

在**原邮件串上 reply**，不要新开邮件（主题自动带 Re:，客服能看到历史）。

```
Dear {客服名或 Sir or Madam},

I am following up on my refund request sent on {首封日期} regarding
Order #{订单号} ({金额}), which I have not yet received a response to.

I understand support teams are busy, but I would appreciate an update
on the status of my request.

If I do not hear back within 5 business days, I will need to
consider other options to resolve this matter.

Thank you for your time.
Best regards,
{姓名}
```

## 规则

- 此封仍不出现 chargeback 字眼——"other options" 已足够传达压力，又不给对方标记你的理由。
- 仍然单诉求：只催退款这一件事。
- 若首封发给了错误地址被退回，换正确地址重发首封模板，而不是用这封。
