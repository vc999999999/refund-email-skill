# 最后通知（跟进后 7–10 天仍无果）

这是整个链路里**唯一**允许提 chargeback 的位置。

```
Dear Sir or Madam,

Despite my refund request of {首封日期} and a follow-up on {跟进日期}
regarding Order #{订单号} ({金额}), I have received no resolution.

I believe this request is reasonable and consistent with your refund
policy. If I do not receive a refund or a substantive response within
7 business days, I will:

1. dispute the charge with my card issuer / PayPal, and
2. file a complaint with the relevant consumer protection authority.

I would prefer to resolve this directly with you and hope we can do so.

Best regards,
{姓名}
({账户邮箱})
```

## 规则

- 发出前和用户确认：列出的行动（拒付、投诉）是用户真的愿意执行的——写了不做，下次就更没信用。
- 措辞保持克制，给对方台阶（"would prefer to resolve this directly with you"）。
- `({账户邮箱})`：在原邮件串 reply 时可省略（对方已有上下文）；若是升级投诉渠道的独立新邮件，必须保留，方便对方查单。
- 仍无回应 → 按 SKILL.md 跟进流程指引用户走银行拒付，并如实说明封号风险。
